The bundled fonts are from Google Fonts (github.com/google/fonts):

- Caveat, Indie Flower, Nothing You Could Do, Reenie Beanie: SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Rock Salt: Apache License 2.0

You can drop any other .ttf/.otf in this folder, including a font made from your own handwriting with a service like Calligraphr. That gives the most convincing results.
